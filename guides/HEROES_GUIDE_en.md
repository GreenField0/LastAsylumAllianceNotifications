# 🦸 Heroes Guide: Squad Building, Synergy & Formation

Heroes are the core of your army. A smartly constructed squad can often defeat opponents with higher overall might! Learn how to upgrade your heroes effectively without wasting precious resources.

---

## 🎯 1. The Golden Rule: Focus on ONE Main Squad

The most common mistake made by beginners is spreading valuable experience (EXP), skill books, and gear across 10 or 15 different heroes.

> [!WARNING]
> **Do not spread your resources thin!**  
> Focus 100% of your upgrade materials on a single **core squad of 5 heroes**. One maxed-out 5-hero squad is infinitely more valuable in PvP and rallies than 15 mediocre heroes!

---

## ⚡ 2. Leverage Faction Synergies

In *Last Asylum*, heroes belong to different factions. Combining multiple heroes from the same faction in your 5-hero lineup unlocks powerful stat bonuses:

| Lineup Combination | Attribute Bonus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **3 Heroes of same faction** | +10% Attack, +10% HP |
| **3 of Faction A + 2 of Faction B** | +15% Attack, +15% HP |
| **4 Heroes of same faction** | +15% Attack, +15% HP, +10% Defense |
| **5 Heroes of same faction** | **+20% Attack, +20% HP, +20% Defense** |

*Tip:* Aim to build a **3+2 squad** or even a **pure 5-hero faction squad** to grab up to 20% free bonus stats!

---

## 🛡️ 3. The Perfect Formation (Frontline vs. Backline)

Your 5 heroes are deployed across two rows: 2 in front (Frontline) and 3 in back (Backline).

```
   [ Frontline / Row 1 ]             [ Backline / Row 2 ]
  🛡️ Tank 1 (e.g., Arthur)     ➔    💥 Carry / Damage Dealer
  🛡️ Tank 2 (e.g., Bella)      ➔    💥 Secondary Damage Dealer
                                ➔    🌿 Support / Healer
```

* **Frontline (Row 1):** Place **only sturdy tanks** here (such as *Arthur* or *Bella*). Their role is to absorb enemy damage and survive so the backline can fight undisturbed.
* **Backline (Row 2):** Place your **damage dealers (Carries/DPS)** and **supporters/healers** here (such as *Marlena*). These heroes deal massive damage but have low survivability.

## 💡 4. Free-to-Play (F2P) Tips

> [!TIP]
> **Use the Alliance Shop!**  
> Do not spend real money just to get strong SSR or UR heroes. By donating daily and participating in alliance events, you earn Alliance Coins. In the **Alliance Shop**, you can regularly buy valuable hero shards (such as for *Bella*) for free to promote your frontline tank to maximum stars!
