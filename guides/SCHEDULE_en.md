# 🛡️ Alliance Schedule: Weekly Overview & Survival Arms Race

Welcome to our official Alliance Schedule! Here you can find all important dates, point sources for the Alliance Duel, and the rotating phases of the Survival Arms Race at a glance.

---

## 🏆 Part 1: Alliance Duel (Weekly Overview)

This table shows exactly what scores points on each day of the week and how best to prepare for the following day to maximize your rewards.

| Day | Focus / Theme | 🎯 Score Points For... | 💡 Prepare For Tomorrow... |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monday**<br>*(Day 1)* | 📦 **Resources & Stamina** | • Consume Stamina<br>• Complete Falcon Quests<br>• Use Antidote<br>• Gather Grain, Wood & Herbs<br>• Consume Raven Fruit & Essence | • Do **not** complete finished building upgrades (leave the 🔨 symbol)<br>• Start new building upgrades that finish tomorrow<br>• Start research projects that take 2 days |
| **Tuesday**<br>*(Day 2)* | 🏗️ **Building & Recruitment** | • Use 1-min Building Speedups<br>• Increase Building Might<br>• Execute 1 UR Covert Operation<br>• Dispatch UR Caravan<br>• Recruit Survivors | • Save Falcon Quests<br>• Start research projects that finish tomorrow |
| **Wednesday**<br>*(Day 3)* | 🔬 **Research & Ravens** | • Complete Falcon Quests<br>• Use 1-min Research Speedups<br>• Consume Study Scrolls<br>• Increase Technology Might<br>• Open Raven Gear Chests | • Do not claim event items or special rewards prematurely |
| **Thursday**<br>*(Day 4)* | 🦸 **Heroes & Shards** | • Use Antidote<br>• Recruit Heroes<br>• Consume UR, SSR & SR Hero Shards<br>• Use Skill Badges | • Save Falcon Quests<br>• Do not complete finished building upgrades (leave 🔨) |
| **Friday**<br>*(Day 5)* | ⚡ **Build, Research & Train** | • Complete Falcon Quests<br>• Use 1-min Speedups (Build/Research/Train)<br>• Increase Building & Tech Might<br>• Train Soldiers | • 🛡️ **Activate Shield!**<br>• Check PvP equipment<br>• Heal troops & prepare attack formations |
| **Saturday**<br>*(Day 6)* | ⚔️ **PvP & Speedups** | • Use 1-min Speedups<br>• Execute 1 UR Covert Operation<br>• Dispatch UR Caravan<br>• Defeat & lose soldiers | • No special preparations required |
| **Sunday** | 🛡️ **Preparation & Rest** | • *No Alliance Duel tasks* | • Save Falcon Quests for Monday<br>• Send resource gatherers (preferably herbs) so they return after 04:00 |

### 💡 Category Legend:
* 🏗️ **Building:** Upgrade and construct buildings
* ⚔️ **Training:** Train and promote soldiers
* 🔬 **Research:** Research technologies in the lab
* 🦅 **Ravens:** Upgrade ravens
* 🦸 **Heroes:** Level up heroes and use shards

## 📌 Important Tips for All Members

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Timing is everything!** Always try to combine the daily theme of the Alliance Duel with the matching 4-hour slot in the Survival Arms Race. This gives you **double rewards** for the exact same effort!

> [!TIP]
> **Leave the Hammer Symbol:** When building points are scheduled for Monday or Friday, start long construction projects days in advance. Once construction finishes, do **not** click the hammer symbol until the exact day that awards points for it!
