# 🦅 Ravens & Epigraphs: The Underrated Power Source

While many players focus only on soldiers and heroes, **Ravens** and the **Epigraph Workshop** are arguably the strongest passive power boosts in the entire game! Learn how to get the maximum out of your feathered companion.

---

## 🪶 1. Why Ravens Are Essential

Ravens are not just decoration for your sanctuary — they provide your troops with permanent, account-wide stat boosts for Attack, Defense, and March Speed.

* **Unlocking:** Clear the plague zones around your sanctuary as early as possible to unlock the **Raven Nest**.
* **Long-Term Investment:** Every level increase of your raven significantly boosts your base Might. Never neglect your Raven Nest!

---

## ⚙️ 2. The Epigraph Workshop

In the Epigraph Workshop, you craft special stones and plans to grant your raven additional skills and attribute bonuses.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Goal: Upgrade your workshop to Level 15 as fast as possible!**  
> At Level 15, you unlock the **Multi-Crafting** feature. This saves you tremendous amounts of time daily and drastically increases your chances of acquiring high-tier epigraphs!

---

## 🧬 3. Raven Evolution & Squad Slots

Using special evolution materials and EXP books, you can evolve your raven.

* **Why evolve?** Evolving your raven unlocks **additional epigraph slots**.
* **The Advantage:** This allows you to apply epigraph plans not just to your first march, but to distribute bonuses across **multiple squads simultaneously**! This is a massive advantage in Server-vs-Server wars and large alliance battles over players who only buff a single squad.
