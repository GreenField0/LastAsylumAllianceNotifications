# 💎 Beginner Tips: Diamonds, Stamina & Construction

Starting out can be tricky — but following these foundational rules will save you weeks of waiting and help you get the absolute maximum out of your account!

---

## 💎 1. Diamond Discipline (The Most Valuable Currency!)

Diamonds are the most precious currency in the game. Wasting them early on will cause you to fall far behind in late-game progression.

> [!WARNING]
> **NEVER waste diamonds on standard construction speedups!**  
> Using diamonds to skip 10 minutes of building time is a pure waste of resources. You get plenty of speedups for free through events and the Alliance Duel!

### What should you spend diamonds on?
1. **VIP Level / VIP Points:** Higher VIP levels grant permanent daily bonuses such as additional builders, faster gathering speeds, and free speedups.
2. **Exclusive Shop Events:** Special items, UR hero shards, or teleporters during limited-time merchant events.
3. **Emergency Shields:** If you ever run out of Alliance Coins on a Friday, buying a 24-hour shield with diamonds is an excellent insurance policy for your troops!

---

## ⚡ 2. Optimal Stamina (Energy) Management

Your stamina regenerates automatically over time. Once the bar reaches its cap (100/100 or 200/200), regeneration stops completely — meaning you are throwing away free energy!

* **Never stay capped:** Use your stamina regularly!
* **Auto-Scavenge:** Use this feature when you are short on time to quickly convert excess stamina into resources and experience.
* **Rallies against Elite Blight:** Participate in alliance rallies as often as possible. They cost very little stamina but provide fantastic rewards for the whole alliance!

---

## 🔬 3. Research Before Building!

Many players focus solely on upgrading their main building (Sanctuary). However, without proper research, your economic engine will stall.

* **Keep the lab running:** There should **always** be an active project in your Research Lab!
* **Prioritize Economy & Military:** First research technologies that increase gathering speed and reduce construction & research times. This pays off every single week during the Alliance Duel!

---

## 🔨 4. The Hammer Trick (For the Alliance Duel)

As described in the [📅 Alliance Schedule](SCHEDULE_en.md): When building might points are scheduled for Monday or Friday, start long construction projects days in advance!
* Once a building upgrade finishes, a **hammer symbol** appears above the building.
* **Do not click it immediately!** The game only counts the building as "completed" at the exact moment you click the hammer symbol. Just leave the symbol floating until the matching duel day and collect massive points effortlessly!
