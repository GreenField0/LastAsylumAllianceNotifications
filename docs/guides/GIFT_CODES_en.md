# 🎁 Redeeming Gift Codes (Especially for iOS / Apple)

Because Apple's App Store policies prohibit the direct entry of gift codes within the game, there is an official workaround for all iPhone and iPad users via an external website. *(Source: [Official Discord Guide](https://discord.com/channels/1417476085179748507/1453210380401639525/1484382932515815558))*

**Android players can also use this link** if they prefer to comfortably redeem codes on their PC!

---

## 🌐 The Official Redemption Website

🔗 **[Last Asylum: Open Official Gift Code Page](https://gevents.globallap.com/gamecode/index.html?gameId=440)**

### How to redeem the code:

1. **Log in:** Log into your account on the website (the easiest way is using your linked Facebook or Google account).
   * *Alternatively:* You can also log in using your **UID (User ID)** and a **One-Time Password**.
2. **Select Server & Character:** Select our server **K225**. Your corresponding character name should appear automatically.
3. **Enter Code:** Type the current redemption code into the field and confirm the redemption.

The rewards will arrive in your in-game mailbox within a few seconds! 💌

---

## 🔍 Where do I find my UID and One-Time Password?

If you prefer not to log in with Facebook/Google, you will need your player ID and a one-time password. Here is how you find both directly in the game:

1. Tap on your **Profile Picture (Doctor Info)** in the top left corner.
2. Go to **Settings** at the bottom.
3. Select **Account**.
4. Tap on **User Center**.

From there, you can copy your UID and generate a one-time password to log in on the website! 😉
