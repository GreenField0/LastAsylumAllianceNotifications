# 📜 Official Alliance Rules & Guidelines

Strong teamwork, mutual respect, and a smart strategy are the keys to our success in *Last Asylum*. Please memorize and follow these basic rules!

---

## 🤝 1. Basic Rules of Conduct

* 🚫 **Do not attack other alliances:** A delicate balance of power rules our home server. Never attack players from other alliances unprovoked!
* 🚫 **No raiding on our home server:** Never attack caravans or execute covert operations against players on our home server!
  * *Tip:* Always direct your covert operations and raids against our current opponents in the **Alliance Duel** (from another server).
* 🏖️ **Report absences:** Real life always comes first! However, if you plan to be absent for an extended period (vacation, illness, exams, business trip, etc.), please inform an **R4 or R5** in advance to prevent an accidental kick for inactivity.
* 🏰 **Raiding Allianceless Sanctuaries (Allowed!):** Sanctuaries and villages on our home server that **do not belong to any alliance** may be freely attacked and looted!
  * 🔍 **Scout first:** Always scout the target before attacking to make sure it has enough resources to be worth the march.
  * 🤝 **Start as an Alliance Rally:** Preferably initiate attacks on allianceless targets as an **Alliance Rally (Versammlung)** rather than a solo march! This allows your fellow guild members to join in, grab loot and resources, and save precious stamina.

## 🚨 2. ALERT: Friday Night / Saturday (State Warfare & SvS)

> [!CAUTION]
> **TL;DR: ACTIVATE YOUR SHIELD ON FRIDAY NIGHT!** 🛡️💥  
> Anyone without an active shield starting Saturday 04:00 (German Time / CET/CEST) will be mercilessly raided by enemy servers and lose their troops in the hospital!

### What happens on Saturday?
Saturday is the day of the great **State Warfare (Server vs. Server / SvS)**. Starting on the night from Friday to Saturday at exactly **04:00 German Time**, borders open: players from enemy servers can teleport to our server and attack sanctuaries to loot points and resources.

### The Golden Shield Rule:
From **Saturday 04:00 to Sunday 04:00 (German Time)**, your sanctuary must be **continuously** protected by an active shield!

### Which shields can you use?
You can use individual shields or combine them by renewing before they expire:
* 🟢 **8-Hour Shield:** Recharges automatically (up to 5 free recharges per week)! Perfect for combining and extending protection.
* 🔵 **12-Hour Shield:** Great for covering half the event duration.
* 🟣 **24-Hour Shield:** The safest and most relaxing option — activate it on Friday before going to sleep and enjoy a worry-free weekend!
* 🟠 **3-Day Shield:** For the extra cautious or if you are away on a weekend trip.
